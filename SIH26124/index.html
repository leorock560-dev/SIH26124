HOW URBANEYE WORKS

From bus camera to field action.

Designed for high-value insights while keeping raw video at the edge.

  1. 01

    Capture

    Bus-mounted cameras observe normal journeys.

  2. 02

    Analyse

    Onboard AI identifies meaningful events locally.

  3. 03

    Transmit

    Only secure, compact alert packets reach the centre.

  4. 04

    Respond

    Officials prioritize and assign action by jurisdiction.
